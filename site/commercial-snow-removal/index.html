Our snow removal process

  1. 1

    Before the storm

    We watch the forecast and prepare. As the storm approaches, we apply brine to keep ice from bonding to your pavement.

  2. 2

    During the storm

    Crews clear lots, entrances, exits and walkways and de-ice as they go, and the phone line stays open if anything changes.

  3. 3

    After the storm

    We apply de-icer to guard against refreezing and improve traction, then document the visit, so your property is ready for the next business day.

Frequently asked questions

When should I set up a commercial snow contract?

The best time is late summer through fall, before the first storm.

Can you handle large parking lots and industrial sites?

We plan equipment and crews around the size of the site. Send us the address and we will tell you how we would cover it.

How do you prove the work was performed?

Every visit is time-stamped and logged through GPS and written work orders, so you have documentation for owners and tenants.

What happens if a storm hits overnight or on a weekend?

Our crews and phone line run 24/7 during weather events so you can reach a real person.
