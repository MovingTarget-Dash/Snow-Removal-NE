Brine vs. rock salt: what is the difference?

Why applying brine before a storm helps keep ice from bonding to pavement, and when salt still makes sense.

What brine does

Brine is salt dissolved in water. Applied before a storm, it helps keep snow and ice from bonding to the pavement, which makes plowing easier and the surface safer.

What rock salt does

Rock salt is applied after snow falls to melt ice and snow already on the surface. It needs moisture to start working and works more slowly in very cold weather.

Using both

Many properties use brine ahead of the storm and salt afterward where ice has formed. Applying the right amount matters: too little leaves ice, too much wastes material and can damage surfaces and plants.

What to ask your contractor

Ask when they apply anti-icing, how they measure the amount of material and how they record each visit.
