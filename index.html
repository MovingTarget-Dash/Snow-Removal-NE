Frequently asked questions

What services do you offer?

Parking lot and driveway plowing, shoveling, sidewalks and entryways, ice control and written snow and ice management plans.

Do you only plow, or do you handle de-icing and salting too?

Both. We plow, shovel, de-ice and salt, so your surfaces are safe to walk and drive on, not just cleared.

When should I set up a snow contract?

The best time is late summer through fall, before the first storm. Most properties are booked before winter, so planning early secures your spot and gives us time to walk your site and build the plan.

How do you prove the work was performed?

Every visit is time-stamped and logged through GPS-tracked equipment and written work orders, and you get updates during the storm, so you have clear documentation for owners, tenants and your own files.

What happens if a storm hits overnight or on a weekend?

Our crews and phone line run 24/7 during weather events, so your property is serviced and you can reach a real person no matter when the snow falls.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Send us the address and we will get back to you with a free quote.
